                6                      DIRECT EXAMINATION

                7   BY MR. BREWER:

                8        Q.   Ms. Ortega, please state your name for the

                9   record.

               10        A.   Maria Lopez Ortega.

               11        Q.   And what is your role at Northline Logistics?

               12        A.   I am the operations manager.

               13        Q.   Why was the delivery delayed that morning?

               14        A.   Because there was so many moving parts on that

               15   route, and nobody had a clear handoff.

               16        Q.   What did you observe when you arrived at the

               17   warehouse lot?

               18        A.   Honestly, there was a lot of cars blocking the

               19   loading bay, so we could not pull in right away.

               20        Q.   Did your drivers communicate with dispatch?

               21        A.   Yes.  They was calling every few minutes asking

               22   for an update.

               23        Q.   Was the incident report already prepared?

               24        A.   Parts of it were drafted earlier, and if it were

               25   to be used at hearing, counsel would finish the blanks.

                                                                           5



                1        Q.   And if it were made as part of an insurance

                2   claim, would that change your testimony today?

                3        A.   No.  My testimony would be the same.

                4        Q.   After the lot cleared, were there still issues?

                5        A.   No.  There were so many workers available by

                6   then that the unload went quickly.

                7        Q.   Thank you.  I have no further questions.

                8             THE COURT REPORTER:  That concludes the

                9        deposition.
